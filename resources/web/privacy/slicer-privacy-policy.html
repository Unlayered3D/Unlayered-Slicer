Our Commitment to Privacy

Unlayered 3D develops software and technology to advance 3D printing, additive manufacturing, computer-aided design, and related engineering applications.

We believe that understanding how people use our software helps us make better products, solve technical problems, and develop new technologies.

We also recognize that 3D printing projects may contain valuable intellectual property, confidential designs, and proprietary manufacturing information.

Your privacy choices matter.

  • Usage diagnostics are optional. You can choose whether to share diagnostic information with Unlayered.
  • Your models stay on your computer unless you choose to upload them. Usage diagnostics do not automatically transmit complete 3D models, CAD files, or G-code.
  • Shared diagnostic information helps us develop technology. When authorized, we may use it to improve our software and conduct research. We use it to develop and train artificial intelligence and other computational systems only for members of the optional Slicing improvement program.
  • You can change your preferences. You can disable optional diagnostics at any time.
  • Our software remains functional if you decline. Standard slicing, exporting, and printer connection functionality remains available.
  • We minimize what we collect. Diagnostic information is restricted to defined technical fields and filtered before transmission.

This policy explains what information Unlayered Slicer processes, how we use it, how long it is retained, and your available privacy rights.

2. Your Choices and Consent

2.1 Optional usage diagnostics

When you first install or update Unlayered Slicer, the application asks whether you want to share optional usage diagnostics.

The screen, titled "Help us build a better slicer", presents two choices:

Keep my data private

No optional usage diagnostics or 3D model data is sent to Unlayered.

Share usage diagnostics (marked Recommended in the United States)

Help us improve reliability and features by sharing application usage, settings, and performance information. Never includes your name, email, account, or 3D models.

The screen also states that you can change your choice at any time in Preferences > Online, and that we do not sell your models or diagnostic data.

In Unlayered Slicer 0.1.6, if you choose United States as your region, "Share usage diagnostics" starts selected, and you can switch to "Keep my data private" before continuing. In all other regions, neither option is preselected. The Slicing improvement program checkbox described in Section 2.7 is never preselected.

No optional diagnostic information is collected or transmitted until you make a choice.

2.2 What sharing authorizes

Where permitted by applicable law, choosing to share diagnostics authorizes Unlayered to collect and process the technical information described in this policy for the disclosed purposes, including:

  • Software reliability, maintenance, and development.
  • Technical troubleshooting and performance analysis.
  • Product improvement and compatibility testing.
  • Research and development of software, hardware, and manufacturing technologies.
  • Statistical analysis of slicing configurations, feature usage, printing outcomes, materials, and machine characteristics.
  • Development of commercial technologies, algorithms, models, and related products.

Sharing usage diagnostics does not authorize the use of your diagnostics to develop, train, test, or evaluate artificial intelligence or machine learning systems. That use is limited to members of the Slicing improvement program described in Section 2.7.

If applicable law requires independent permission for particular processing purposes, Unlayered will obtain that permission before conducting the affected processing.

Sharing diagnostics does not authorize uploading your actual 3D models, CAD files, or project geometry.

Those uploads require separate authorization.

2.3 Changing your choice

You can change your diagnostic preference through:

Preferences > Online > Send usage diagnostics

When you disable diagnostics:

  • Collection of new optional diagnostic events stops.
  • No additional optional diagnostic batches are transmitted.
  • Diagnostic events queued locally but not yet transmitted are deleted.
  • Standard application functionality remains available.

Information already received may remain subject to the retention periods and processing purposes described in this policy, as limited by your applicable privacy rights and legal requirements.

2.4 Previously installed versions

If you disabled diagnostics in an earlier version, that preference remains disabled when upgrading.

Users whose prior settings do not establish a valid preference for the current notice will be asked to make a choice before optional collection begins.

2.5 Organization-wide control

Organizations may disable optional diagnostics on managed computers through the environment variable:

UNLAYERED_NO_TELEMETRY

When enabled, this control overrides the application's diagnostic preference and prevents optional diagnostic collection and transmission.

2.6 Changes requiring additional permission

If Unlayered materially changes the categories of information collected or the purposes for which information is processed, we will update the relevant disclosures and obtain additional permission where required by law.

2.7 Slicing improvement program

When you select "Share usage diagnostics", an additional checkbox appears: Slicing improvement program. It is not selected by default in any region.

Its text reads: "Get early access to new features. Your shared data, never linked to your name, email, or account, may be used to develop, train, test, evaluate, and improve AI, CAD, slicing, software, manufacturing, and hardware systems."

If you join the program, your usage diagnostics, consisting of the same information described in Section 3 (including slicing settings, numerical part measurements, and print ratings), may be used to research, develop, train, test, evaluate, and improve artificial intelligence, machine learning, CAD, slicing, software, manufacturing, and hardware systems.

In a future version of Unlayered Slicer, program members will also share 3D model geometry. Unlayered will notify members and ask for their permission again before any geometry is collected. Unlayered Slicer 0.1.6 does not collect model geometry.

Joining the program is optional. You can leave it at any time in Preferences > Online. Switching usage diagnostics off also ends your membership. Leaving the program does not affect standard application features.

Early access

Program members receive early access to new Unlayered Slicer features before they are generally available. Early access is available only while you are a member of the program.

3. Information Collected Through Usage Diagnostics

If you authorize optional diagnostics, Unlayered Slicer may transmit the information described below.

Diagnostic events are created from defined fields, using an allowlist intended to exclude information not needed for the specified technical purposes.

3.1 Application and device information

This may include:

  • Application version and release channel.
  • Operating system and version.
  • Processor architecture.
  • Processor core count.
  • Installed memory, rounded to whole gigabytes.
  • Application language.
  • Whether the application is being launched for the first time.
  • Display preferences, including dark mode and stealth mode.
  • Status and version of the optional Bambu Lab network plug-in.

This information helps us understand compatibility, software performance, and technical issues across different systems.

3.2 Application and feature usage

We may collect information about how application features are used, including:

  • Features and application tabs opened.
  • Built-in printer, process, and material presets selected.
  • Categories of files imported, such as STL or 3MF.
  • Types of exports performed.
  • Application session duration.
  • Software update activity.
  • General outcomes of account sign-in attempts.

Diagnostic events do not include custom file names, user-defined preset names, printer network addresses, or account credentials.

3.3 Slicing configurations and parameters

For individual slicing operations, we may collect:

  • Printer manufacturer or vendor.
  • Material types.
  • Built-in preset identifiers or general custom-preset classifications.
  • Enabled slicing features.
  • Slicing settings changed from built-in defaults.
  • Values of permitted settings, up to 60 modified settings per slice.
  • Number of plates, objects, and copies.
  • Estimated printing time.
  • Estimated model and support-material volumes.
  • Time required to perform the slicing operation.
  • Whether slicing succeeded, failed, or was canceled.

This information allows us to understand how users configure slicing operations and how those configurations relate to technical performance.

Settings are restricted to permitted fields, and individual values are limited to 80 characters.

Custom G-code, post-processing scripts, credentials, file paths, private notes, and other excluded free-form values are not included in diagnostic settings.

3.4 Numerical part characteristics

To better understand how geometry affects slicing and manufacturing, Unlayered may collect numerical measurements describing up to 20 parts associated with an individual slicing operation.

These measurements may include:

  • Bounding-box dimensions.
  • Estimated part volume.
  • Surface area.
  • Percentage of overhanging surface.
  • Percentage of surface contacting the build plate.
  • Number of copies.

These values describe certain characteristics of a part without transmitting the complete geometry.

Although numerical measurements are not complete CAD geometry, they may reveal information about a proprietary design.

Users working with confidential or sensitive engineering projects should consider this when deciding whether to share diagnostics.

3.5 Slicing errors

If slicing fails, we may collect:

  • Error category.
  • Sanitized error message.
  • General operation status.
  • Slicing duration and result.

Error messages are restricted to their first line, limited to 160 characters, and filtered to remove known private text, file paths, and project or object names.

For errors involving custom G-code templates, only the error category is transmitted.

3.6 Application crashes

When an unexpected application exit is detected, diagnostic information may be transmitted during the next launch.

This information may include:

  • Application version.
  • General application activity at the time of failure.
  • Approximate session duration.
  • Exception code or signal.
  • Program module and offset.
  • Since version 0.1.10, up to 256 KB of the end of the session log of the session that ended unexpectedly. Your name, folders (your home folder), file names, email addresses, network addresses, computer name and secrets (passwords, keys and tokens) are removed on your device before it is sent, and again on arrival. The log may still contain the names of printers and presets. It is sent to unlayered3d.com/api/slicer/crash-log with a random report identifier that links it to that crash report only, kept up to 90 days, and only Unlayered staff can access it.

Memory dumps, models, project files and unfiltered application logs are not transmitted.

If you do not share usage diagnostics, this information is sent only if you agree: after an unexpected exit, the next launch asks whether to send a crash report, shows what it contains, and sends it once with a random identifier that is not linked to your installation. If you decline, or close the question, nothing is sent, and the log is not read.

3.7 Print results and user feedback

Unlayered may offer an optional prompt asking users to describe the outcome of a print.

If a user provides feedback while diagnostics are enabled, we may collect:

  • Whether the print was successful or experienced a problem.
  • Selected problem categories, such as warping or stringing.
  • Whether G-code was exported or sent.
  • A random identifier linking the feedback to the relevant slicing operation.

This information can help us understand relationships between slicing configurations, part characteristics, material selection, and printing results.

3.8 Software updates and sign-in outcomes

Authorized diagnostics may include:

  • Whether an update was offered.
  • Whether an update was downloaded.
  • Whether an update was successfully installed.
  • General categories of update failures.
  • Whether an account sign-in attempt succeeded or failed.

Optional diagnostic events do not include account passwords, email addresses, or authentication tokens.

Actual account authentication is handled separately as described in Section 6.

6. Network Communications Outside Optional Diagnostics

Certain application functions involve network communications regardless of your diagnostics preference.

6.1 Software update checks

The application checks for software updates.

Update requests include:

  • Application version.
  • Operating system.
  • Processor architecture.

When diagnostics are enabled, update requests may also include the installation identifier to support installation counting and update analysis.

When diagnostics are disabled, that identifier is not included in the update request.

Whatever your choice, two update requests also carry one extra item each: the first one after an update installs says which version it updated from, and the first one after you make or change your privacy choice says which choice you made (share usage diagnostics, or keep my data private). We only count these; we do not store them with the installation identifier or with an account. If your organization has switched diagnostics off on your computer (Section 2.5), neither is sent.

6.2 Account authentication

If you sign in to an Unlayered account, authentication is processed through our account infrastructure and is subject to our website privacy policy.

6.3 Printer connections

Unlayered does not ordinarily receive the contents of those direct connections.

6.4 Third-party plug-ins

6.5 Website and Universe features

7. Identifiers and Pseudonymous Data

Authorized diagnostics may use three randomly generated identifiers.

Installation ID

Identifies an application installation and allows events from that installation to be associated over time.

Session ID

Identifies an application session.

Slice ID

Identifies an individual slicing operation and allows print feedback to be linked to that operation.

These identifiers are not generated from hardware serial numbers, account credentials, or personal contact information.

Because an installation identifier can connect multiple records, the resulting diagnostic information is pseudonymous, not necessarily anonymous.

Unlayered does not intentionally link optional diagnostic records to user accounts, names, or email addresses.

The identifiers may nevertheless constitute personal information under applicable law.

The installation ID is a random UUID created on your computer the first time you choose to share diagnostics. It is stored in the application's configuration as usage_diagnostics_id and is sent only while diagnostics are enabled. If you switch diagnostics off, the identifier remains stored on your computer so that you can quote it in a privacy request. The installation ID is not rotated.

8. Collection, Transmission, and Storage

8.1 Diagnostic transmission

Authorized diagnostic events are sent using HTTPS to:

https://unlayered3d.com/api/slicer/telemetry

Events are generally transmitted periodically and when the application closes.

If transmission is unsuccessful, up to 20 pending batches may be retained temporarily in the application's local data directory.

Pending batches may be retried when the application next launches.

Disabling diagnostics removes unsent pending events.

8.2 Local filtering

Before transmission, the application applies an allowlist specifying permitted events and fields.

Unexpected fields are discarded.

Settings and error information are filtered to exclude defined categories of potentially private information.

8.3 Service providers

Our documented diagnostic infrastructure includes:

Vercel

Provides hosting and API request processing for unlayered3d.com.

Upstash

Provides Redis database infrastructure for diagnostic counts, recent event batches, and retained development records.

We may use additional service providers to operate, maintain, secure, or improve our services.

Where these providers process personal information on our behalf, we will apply contractual and legal safeguards as required.
