Bambu Lab printers
The Bambu Lab network plug-in is required for wireless communication with Bambu Lab 3D printers: sending prints over Wi-Fi or LAN and seeing the printer's status and camera.
It is made by Bambu Lab, not Unlayered, and is downloaded when you press Finish. Using a Bambu Lab printer? Tick the box - you can also install it later from Preferences.
