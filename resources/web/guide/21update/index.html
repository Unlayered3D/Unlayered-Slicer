Update your Unlayered printer
Get the latest firmware and printer settings for your Unlayered printer. Connect it to your network, then press Check for updates. We install the firmware first, then the settings; you confirm each one. Your calibration, name and network settings are never replaced.
Downloading the printer settings needs your free Unlayered account. Sign in
Check for updates
Later, you find this in the Prepare tab: press Connection (the signal icon next to Printer), then Firmware or Config. When an update is published, Unlayered Slicer also shows a "Printer update available" notification.
