# Nonplanar usability and planning work

The owner requested these improvements on October 16, 2026. The baseline remains
the validated travel build `71c29186` until a replacement has independent build,
native, packaged-runtime and graphical-startup checks. The first physical test
is the unsliced pair of small 4-degree ramps; the owner slices and prints it.

## Current source changes

- Underside analysis: elevated downward facets, bed clipping, bounded cardinal
  and diagonal layer-direction comparisons, configured-angle/reference labels,
  area-weighted angular-deficit and opposing-overhang regression reports. Saved
  selection reasons distinguish hypothetical directions from applied fields.
  When no roof patch fits, a bounded underside field can regenerate Normal
  (auto) supports with the solid. A flat foundation, affine bridge slabs and
  generated support/bridge anchoring checks are required before export.
- Layer-mode Preview: explicit native planar/transition/nonplanar tags, a colour
  legend, local inclination in the position inspector, and expandable selection
  decisions. Old files without mode tags are not assigned invented transitions.
- Portable review builds: retain compilation, stock catalog checks, portable
  artifact and native tests while skipping installer, PDB and Store packaging.
  Normal release callers retain their previous packaging defaults.
- Deployment helpers now use a local per-build data directory and single-instance
  preferences. The baseline GUI was opened and repeat-launch forwarding checked
  after the shared OneDrive preset-cache startup failure. No slicing or physical
  print was initiated by the agent.

These source changes do not yet constitute a delivered replacement package.

## Remaining authorized work

1. Compare actual planar and curved candidates for benefit, time, material and
   travel cost; expose finish/balanced/time preferences without claiming measured
   finish from a mesh heuristic.
2. Preserve seam continuity and extrusion starts through changing fields and
   topology, while respecting painted/manual seam constraints where supported.
3. Improve conforming to eligible curved roofs with bounded shape and spacing
   error, rather than merely adjusting independently tapered local directions.
4. Plan local cleared travel routes from deposited geometry, with global fallback
   when the local envelope or path certificate is unavailable.
5. Support additional ordinary modifiers, painting and variable-height workflows
   after their physical-coordinate semantics are defined and verified.
6. Add a user-operated comparison workflow with separate planar and nonplanar
   previews/estimates; preserve the original project and do not export a job.
7. Support-aware deposition planning and measured toolhead-shape swept-volume
   checks are larger engine features. Neither support removal nor an all-printer
   safety claim follows from selecting a roof field.

Physical results, geometry certificates, software checks and UI observations
remain separate evidence. No new clearance values, printer profiles or nozzle
limits should be inferred while implementing these features.
